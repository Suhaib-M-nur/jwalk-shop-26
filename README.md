# J BAO · Shop 26 · J WALK

Interactive, walkable 3D model of Shop 26 at J WALK (Al-Jammawat Park, Madinah), fitted out as a J BAO showroom.

**Open it:** https://suhaib-m-nur.github.io/jwalk-shop-26/

- **Walk around:** the page opens walking, just inside the entrance. Use W A S D or the arrow keys and drag to look (on phones, the on-screen stick). Exit for the orbit views.
- **Two modes:** the J BAO showroom, or the unit as it is now (bare shell, exposed services, shutters as in the site photo).
- **Plan:** the 120 m² sales floor, back-wall windows and columns come from the shop's DWG. The glass display room (7.5 m²) and the black shopfront (two openings, about 3.80 m and 2.60 m) follow the site photo.
- **Fit-out:** styled after the Blueage stores: white plaster ceiling with lightboxes and white track spots, cream porcelain floor, backlit plaster wall panels on slotted uprights, white floor rails and two-tier tables, an oak fluted wall behind the cashier with halo-lit orange J BAO letters, and security gates in J BAO orange printed with the logo.
- **Merchandise:** every garment, shoe, bag, cap, folded stack, mannequin, till and shopping bag is a 3D model generated with Higgsfield (seven garment styles, four mannequins), as are the plaster, stone, oak and walnut textures and the campaign posters. Corner shading and a soft glow on the lights are added on desktop. In the showroom the wall panels cover the two back-wall windows.
- **Outside:** the palm plaza seen through the entrances is an illustrative photo backdrop, not the surveyed surroundings.

A single self-contained `index.html`; three.js loads from a public CDN.
